# Demand Management

## Objective

Demand Management represents the planned requirement for the finished product and provides the basis for production planning.

For this simulation, demand is defined for finished product **FG-1000**.

## Demand Scenario

| Material | Planned Demand | Unit | Planning Period |
|---|---:|---|---|
| FG-1000 | 100 | EA | Month 1 |

## Planning Purpose

The planned demand is used as an input to the material planning process.

The simulation uses the demand requirement to determine whether the required components are available or need to be planned for procurement or production.

## Relationship with PP Process

Demand  
→ MRP  
→ Material Requirements  
→ Production Planning  
→ Production Order

## Planning Assumption

The simulated manufacturing business expects a requirement of **100 units of FG-1000** during the defined planning period.

## Scope Note

The demand values in this document are simulated project data created for self-directed learning and portfolio demonstration. They do not represent actual customer demand or production planning data.
