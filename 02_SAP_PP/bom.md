
# Bill of Material (BOM)

## Objective

The Bill of Material (BOM) defines the components required to manufacture the finished product.

For this simulation, finished product **FG-1000** requires three raw materials.

## BOM Structure

| Finished Product | Component | Quantity | Unit |
|---|---|---:|---|
| FG-1000 | RM-1001 | 2 | EA |
| FG-1000 | RM-1002 | 1 | EA |
| FG-1000 | RM-1003 | 1 | EA |

## Component Details

- **RM-1001:** Raw Material A
- **RM-1002:** Raw Material B
- **RM-1003:** Raw Material C
- **FG-1000:** Finished Product

## Example

To manufacture **1 unit of FG-1000**, the simulation requires:

- 2 units of RM-1001
- 1 unit of RM-1002
- 1 unit of RM-1003

## Process Relationship

The BOM provides the component structure used later in the production planning and MRP process.

Material Master → BOM → Routing → Production Version → MRP → Production Order

## Scope Note

This BOM is a project-scenario dataset created for self-directed learning and portfolio demonstration. It is not taken from an actual client or production SAP system.
