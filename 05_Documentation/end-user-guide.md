# End User Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides a simple overview of the simulated SAP S/4HANA PP & QM manufacturing process.

## Manufacturing Process

### Step 1 — Review Material Master

Review the finished product and raw-material information.

### Step 2 — Review BOM

Verify the components required to manufacture the finished product.

### Step 3 — Review Routing

Verify the sequence of manufacturing operations.

### Step 4 — Review Production Version

Verify that the appropriate BOM and routing are connected.

### Step 5 — Review Demand

Confirm the planned requirement for the finished product.

### Step 6 — Review MRP

Review the calculated material requirements based on demand and the BOM.

### Step 7 — Review Production Order

Review the manufacturing quantity and production information.

### Step 8 — Execute Production

Complete the defined manufacturing operations and record production confirmation.

### Step 9 — Record Goods Receipt

Record the completed finished-product quantity.

### Step 10 — Perform Quality Inspection

Evaluate the manufactured product against the defined quality characteristics.

### Step 11 — Record Inspection Results

Record the results of the quality checks.

### Step 12 — Make Usage Decision

Evaluate the inspection results and make the appropriate usage decision.

## End-to-End Flow

Demand
→ MRP
→ Production Order
→ Production Execution
→ Goods Receipt
→ Quality Inspection
→ Inspection Results
→ Usage Decision

## User Checklist

- [ ] Material information reviewed
- [ ] BOM verified
- [ ] Routing verified
- [ ] Production version verified
- [ ] Demand reviewed
- [ ] MRP requirements reviewed
- [ ] Production order reviewed
- [ ] Production completed
- [ ] Goods receipt completed
- [ ] Quality inspection completed
- [ ] Inspection results recorded
- [ ] Usage decision completed

## Scope Note

This guide is part of a self-directed SAP S/4HANA PP & QM portfolio simulation. It describes the simulated business process and does not represent an actual end-user guide for a production SAP system.
